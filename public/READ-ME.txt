BLOSSOM SCHOOL — INTERACTIVE 3D MODEL

Open Blossom-School-3D.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
The viewer is self-contained and works without an internet connection.

CONTROLS
The campus rotates automatically. Use Pause / Resume to control rotation.
Drag to look around the campus. Scroll or pinch to zoom.
Right-drag or use two fingers to pan.
Use the School, Left-side dam and Blossom gate buttons for closer views.
Use Open gate to swing the gate leaves. Use Top view to inspect the layout.

YOUR REQUESTED CHANGES
The dam is on the left when looking at the front of the school.
The upper and lower roads connect along the right side.
The entrance has moved from the front wall to the right boundary.
Cherry blossom trees and flowering vines surround the new gate.

FILES
Blossom-School-3D.html — interactive, rotating scene with moving water and traffic.
Blossom-School.glb — portable 3D asset with embedded textures and a 30-second
                    full-turn animation named Campus 360 turntable.
Blossom-School-360.mp4 — a full-turn video preview.
Blossom-School-preview.png — overview image.

This is a visual reconstruction using the supplied model photograph and school
photographs. Dimensions and surfaces hidden in the photographs are inferred.
The GLB uses the EXT_mesh_gpu_instancing extension for efficient repeated detail.
Water flow, traffic motion, interactive gate opening and drifting petals are
animated in the HTML viewer; the GLB contains the campus turntable animation.
